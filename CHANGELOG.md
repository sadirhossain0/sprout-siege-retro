# Changelog

## 1.0.0: First release
- Sprout Siege 2.2 redrawn as pixel art: 60 levels in 6 worlds, 18 plants, 15 zombies with their own tricks, the Compost Giant boss, stars and the Daily Challenge.
- Pixel sizes: Chunky, Classic and Fine.
- Color palettes: Arcade 16, Handheld Green, Pocket Gray, Amber Monitor, Sunset 8 and 16-bit Color, with optional dithering.
- CRT scanlines, chiptune sound, a pixel font, difficulty levels and a screen shake switch.
- Its own save, separate from the regular game.
