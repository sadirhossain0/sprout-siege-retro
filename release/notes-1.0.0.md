## Sprout Siege Retro 1.0

The garden-defense game Sprout Siege, redrawn as chunky pixel art with chiptune sound. All 60 levels, 18 plants, 15 zombies and the Compost Giant boss are here, along with the Daily Challenge and star ratings.

### What makes it retro
- **Pixel graphics:** the game is drawn at a low resolution and scaled up with hard pixel edges. Pick **Chunky** (320x180), **Classic** (427x240) or **Fine** (640x360) pixels.
- **6 color palettes:** Arcade 16, Handheld Green, Pocket Gray, Amber Monitor, Sunset 8 and 16-bit Color, with optional ordered dithering.
- **CRT scanlines** that line up with the pixel rows.
- **Chiptune sound:** every sound effect and song is played on square and triangle waves through a 4-bit crusher.
- **Pixel font** that stays sharp at any pixel size.
- **Difficulty** (Easy, Normal, Hard) and a **Screen shake** switch.

Find all of these in **Options → Retro Settings**. The retro edition keeps its own save, separate from the regular game.
